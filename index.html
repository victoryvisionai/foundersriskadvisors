What we don't do

Your client stays your client.

  • No cross-selling. We don't approach your client about benefits, wealth, or any line you already hold.
  • No going direct. Introductions come through you, and renewals go back through you.
  • No surprises on economics. The split is agreed in writing before the first client conversation.
  • No case we can't do well. If it isn't a fit, we say so and hand it back the same week.
